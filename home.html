What's inside

It brings the tools families and clinicians already trust into one standalone device.

Standalone by design. A dedicated hardware device, built that way for safety.

  • Social stories

    Customised social stories.

  • About me

    User profiles about your child.

  • Visual routine boards

  • Timers

    To help with waits.

  • Calming activities

    Activities that up-regulate or down-regulate, depending on your child's needs.

  • Feelings board

    So your child can talk about and process their feelings, connect with you and communicate.

  • Pain scale board

    With visuals to identify what their pain is and communicate it.

  • A mascot that speaks their language

    A little mascot throughout talks to your child in the language that best suits them, like English or Chinese.

  • The language of their support needs

    Declarative language for a child with severe PDA. Safety and autonomy language around food for a child with OCD or fear-based needs.
