What’s inside

It brings the tools families and clinicians already trust into one standalone device.

Standalone by design. A dedicated hardware device, built that way for safety.

  • Social stories

    Customised, like getting a blood test.

  • About me

    User profiles about the patient.

  • Daily living

    Support for activities of daily living during admissions and ED visits.

  • Visual routine boards

    Each step, one picture at a time.

  • Staff profiles

    The people caring for them.

  • Procedures

    Information about procedures.

  • Daily flow

    Today, it’s an X-ray.

  • Timers

    To help with waits.

  • Calming activities

    Activities that up-regulate or down-regulate, depending on the patient’s needs.

  • Feelings board

    To talk about and process feelings, connect with caregivers and communicate.

  • Pain scale board

    With visuals to identify what the pain is and communicate it.

  • Speaks their language

    A mascot that talks to the patient in the language that best suits them, like English or Chinese.

  • Speaks to their needs

    Declarative language for PDA. Safety and autonomy language around food for OCD or fear-based needs.

Set up for EDs and wards, eating disorder clinics with menu options, and physio programs with each patient’s activities.
